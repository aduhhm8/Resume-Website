Meet Adam

Hi, I'm Adam. I'm a Computer Science major at California State University, Long Beach, with a concentration in cybersecurity.


My path into tech started with my favorite calculus course and favortie professor at Cypress College. Working through problems there got me curious about code, and that curiosity turned into real project ideas, like a password manager with encrypted storage. That's what eventually pulled me toward security.


In summer 2024 I joined the CIC PCUBED Summer Research Project at Cal State Fullerton. There I wrote code to analyze real stock data, built our presentation, and presented the work. Since then I've been focused on learning security hands on, and I'm currently designing a password manager with encrypted storage.


Outside of class, I'm the Outreach Chair for my NSBE chapter, where I genuinely love getting to know my peers and building my leadership skills. I also work full time as a barista at Nordstrom, where I was recognized as an All Star Employee.
